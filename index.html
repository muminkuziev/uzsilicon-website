integratsiyalashgan sanoat platformasi

Building a multi-partner industrial pathway from validated quartz and silicon metal to captive polysilicon and solar-grade monocrystalline wafers. Tasdiqlangan kvarts va metallurgik kremniydan ichki polisilikon va quyosh darajasidagi monokristalli waferlarga qadar ko'p hamkorli sanoat yo'lini quramiz.

Uz Silicon Tech is a proposed industrial platform designed to connect resource validation, competitive energy, silicon metal, polysilicon, crystal growth and wafer manufacturing in Uzbekistan, supported by selected international wafer JV platforms. Uz Silicon Tech — O'zbekistonda resurslarni tekshirish, raqobatbardosh energiya, metallurgik kremniy, polisilikon, kristall o'stirish va wafer ishlab chiqarishni bog'lash uchun taklif etilayotgan sanoat platformasi bo'lib, tanlangan xalqaro wafer JV platformalari bilan qo'llab-quvvatlanadi.

Strategic thesisStrategik tezis

Wafers are the strategic output — not polysilicon sales.Strategik mahsulot — wafer, polysilicon savdosi emas.

The platform is conceived around solar-grade monocrystalline silicon wafers. Upstream polysilicon would serve predominantly as captive feedstock for crystal growth and wafer production, not as a standalone commodity business.Platforma quyosh darajasidagi monokristalli kremniy waferlariga yo'naltirilgan. Yuqori zanjirdagi polisilikon asosan kristall o'stirish va wafer ishlab chiqarish uchun ichki xomashyo bo'ladi, mustaqil tovar biznesi emas.

Wafer-first positioningWafer-birinchi yo'nalish

Core capacity logicAsosiy sig'im mantig'i

Conceptual scale, not confirmed capacity.Konseptual ko'lam, tasdiqlangan quvvat emas.

100 kt/y

Up to 100,000 tonnes per year is a conceptual upstream scale — not an approved plant capacity. Its strategic role is predominantly to supply prospective wafer operations rather than open-market polysilicon sales.Yiliga 100 000 tonnagacha — tasdiqlangan zavod quvvati emas, konseptual yuqori zanjir ko'lami. Uning roli asosan ochiq bozor polysilicon savdosi emas, kelajakdagi wafer operatsiyalarini ta'minlashdir.

45–55 GW/y

Approximately 45–55 GW per year is an indicative conversion range. Actual output depends on product format, wafer thickness, kerf loss, process yield, technology choice and qualification.Yiliga taxminan 45–55 GW — indikativ diapazon. Haqiqiy hajm mahsulot formati, wafer qalinligi, kesish yo'qotishi, unum, texnologiya va kvalifikatsiyaga bog'liq.

Both figures are conceptual target-scale estimates only — subject to Gate 1 validation, quartz / feedstock quality, technology selection, mass balance, wafer thickness and kerf loss, process yield, engineering, electricity availability, water, partner structure, financing, qualification and market conditions. Neither figure should be read as approved, financed, contracted or under construction. Har ikkala ko'rsatkich faqat konseptual maqsad-ko'lam bahosi — Gate 1 tekshiruvi, kvarts / xomashyo sifati, texnologiya tanlovi, moddiy balans, wafer qalinligi va kesish yo'qotishi, jarayon unumi, muhandislik, elektr energiyasi mavjudligi, suv, hamkorlik tuzilmasi, moliyalashtirish, kvalifikatsiya va bozor sharoitiga bog'liq. Hech biri tasdiqlangan, moliyalashtirilgan, shartnoma qilingan yoki qurilayotgan deb o'qilmasligi kerak.
