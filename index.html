Integrated Silicon-to-Wafer Industrial PlatformKremniydan wafergacha integratsiyalashgan sanoat platformasi

A proposed multi-partner industrial platform designed to connect validated quartz resources, competitive energy, silicon metal, polysilicon and solar-grade monocrystalline wafer manufacturing across Uzbekistan and selected international JV locations.

O‘zbekiston va tanlangan xalqaro JV hududlarida tasdiqlangan kvarts resurslari, raqobatbardosh energiya, metallurgik kremniy, polisilikon va quyosh darajasidagi monokristalli wafer ishlab chiqarishni bog‘lash uchun taklif etilayotgan ko‘p hamkorli sanoat platformasi.

Strategic thesisStrategik tezis

Wafers are the strategic output.Strategik mahsulot — wafer.

The platform is conceived around solar-grade monocrystalline silicon wafers—not commodity polysilicon sales. Upstream polysilicon would serve predominantly as captive feedstock for crystal growth and wafer production.

Platforma oddiy polisilikon savdosi emas, quyosh darajasidagi monokristalli kremniy waferlariga yo‘naltirilgan. Polisilikon asosan kristall o‘stirish va wafer ishlab chiqarish uchun ichki xomashyo bo‘ladi.

Specialist work packages allow geological, energy, technology, engineering, manufacturing, financing and market-access participants to be evaluated independently.

Ixtisoslashgan ish paketlari geologiya, energiya, texnologiya, muhandislik, ishlab chiqarish, moliyalashtirish va bozorga kirish ishtirokchilarini mustaqil baholash imkonini beradi.

100 kt/y

Captive polysilicon logicIchki polisilikon mantig‘i

Up to 100,000 tonnes per year is a conceptual upstream scale—not an approved plant capacity. Its strategic role is predominantly to supply prospective wafer operations.

Yiliga 100 000 tonnagacha — tasdiqlangan zavod quvvati emas, konseptual yuqori zanjir ko‘lami. Uning roli asosan kelajakdagi wafer operatsiyalarini ta’minlashdir.

45–55 GW/y

Indicative wafer potentialIndikativ wafer salohiyati

Approximately 45–55 GW per year is an indicative conversion range. Actual output depends on product format, wafer thickness, kerf loss, process yield, technology choice and qualification.

Yiliga taxminan 45–55 GW — indikativ diapazon. Haqiqiy hajm mahsulot formati, wafer qalinligi, kesish yo‘qotishi, unum, texnologiya va kvalifikatsiyaga bog‘liq.
